Our Purpose

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To bridge cultures and communities through the universal language of food. We strive to serve the most authentic, hand-crafted dumplings and bao, honoring traditional cooking techniques while embracing local ingredients. Every meal we serve is a testament to our dedication to culinary excellence.

Our Vision

To be recognized globally as the premier destination for authentic Asian comfort food, creating welcoming spaces where friends and families can gather to share joy, warmth, and unforgettable flavors. We envision a future where our culinary heritage continues to inspire and connect people everywhere.

Timeline

A Journey of Flavor

1998

The Humble Beginnings

Grandma Lee opened a tiny food stall in the night market, selling her signature hand-pleated pork buns to locals.

2010

First Restaurant Opens

Moving from the streets to our first brick-and-mortar location in the heart of downtown, expanding our daily menu.

2026

Award-Winning Chain

Now operating three branches, recognized city-wide for preserving traditional dumpling craftsmanship and quality.

What Drives Us

Our Core Values

Authenticity

Staying true to our roots without taking shortcuts. Every recipe is honored exactly as it was created decades ago by our ancestors.

Passion

We pour our hearts into every dumpling we fold. Cooking isn't just a job for us; it's a lifelong dedication to the culinary arts.

Community

Food brings people together. We aim to create a welcoming environment where our guests feel like part of our extended family.

Quality Sourcing

Farm to Dumpling

We believe that world-class food starts with world-class ingredients. That's why we partner exclusively with local farmers and sustainable meat purveyors within a 50-mile radius to ensure maximum freshness.

  • 100% Organic, Local Vegetables
  • Ethically Raised, Hormone-Free Meats
  • Zero Artificial Preservatives or MSG
