Service FAQ

Common Inquiries

How far in advance do I need to book catering?

For orders under 50 people, we require 48 hours notice. For larger events or custom menus, please contact us at least two weeks in advance.

Do you offer vegetarian and gluten-free options?

Yes! We have a dedicated selection of vegetable dumplings, tofu bao, and rice noodle dishes. Please inform us of any severe allergies when booking.

Does catering include setup and staff?

Drop-off catering does not include staff. However, our "Custom Event Menu" packages can include professional servers, bartenders, and on-site chefs for an additional fee.

How many dumplings should I order per person?

We generally recommend 6-8 dumplings per person if served as an appetizer, and 12-15 pieces per person if served as a main course.
